# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering leaders, senior engineers, and tech recruiters evaluating Piyush as a candidate, collaborator, or advisor. Arriving from LinkedIn, GitHub, or a conference talk. Skimming in 90 seconds or reading deeply if the first signal hooks them. Context: desktop or mobile, high intent, low patience.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and engineering blog for Piyush Gupta, Sr Engineering Manager (ex-Hotstar/Disney, Slice). The site proves two things simultaneously: that he thinks at the architecture/system level (EM credibility) AND that he can still build (practitioner credibility). The blog posts are the primary proof vehicle — they are case studies, not opinion pieces. Success = a recruiter forwarding the URL, or an engineering leader booking a call.

## Brand Personality

Precise, opinionated, practitioner. Voice: a staff engineer who became a manager and didn't lose the calluses.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS "dark mode with neon" portfolio (Tailwind starter vibes)
- Medium-blog simplicity with no visual system
- Consultant-portfolio polish that reads as too smooth, no edge
- Resume-as-website (static list of jobs, no proof of thinking)

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not the claim.** Diagrams, metrics, and real git output beat bullets like "led a team of 12."
2. **Practitioner energy.** The aesthetic should feel like a tool, not a brochure. Precision over decoration.
3. **One strong signal per section.** Don't compete with yourself. Each scroll stop has one job.
4. **Earn the credibility.** Trust signals (metrics, company logos, post depth) should feel discovered, not announced.
5. **Mobile is a first read.** A recruiter checking on their phone mid-commute is the primary mobile user — they need the hook fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Keyboard navigable. Screen reader compatible headings. No motion that can't be reduced.
